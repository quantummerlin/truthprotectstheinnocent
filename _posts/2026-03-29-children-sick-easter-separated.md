## Prof. Cantelmi Publishes Catherine's Night of Anguish

Prof. Tonino Cantelmi — the acclaimed psychiatrist who previously released findings confirming the children are suffering irreparable psychological harm every day — has now shared, via Italian journalist **Marina Terragni**, an account of a devastating night in Catherine's ordeal.
