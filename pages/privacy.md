# Privacy Policy & Data Handling

**Last updated:** March 11, 2026

This page explains how the *Truth Protects The Innocent* campaign collects, processes, and publishes data, with particular reference to audience intelligence reports and public comment analysis.

---

## Data Source

Audience sentiment data and public comment analysis displayed on this website is generated by **[ai.quantummerlin.com](https://ai.quantummerlin.com)**, an audience intelligence platform. The source material consists of **publicly visible comments** posted on **public Facebook posts** by users who chose to comment voluntarily with their real names visible.

---

## Legal Framework

Three overlapping legal frameworks govern how we handle this data:

| Framework | Scope |
|---|---|
| **GDPR** (General Data Protection Regulation) | Applies because commenters include EU citizens |
| **Italian Privacy Law** (Codice della Privacy / D.Lgs. 196/2003) | The Italian implementation of GDPR |
| **Facebook Terms of Service** | Governs what can be done with data from the platform |

Our data handling is conducted under **GDPR Article 6(1)(f), Legitimate Interests**, balancing the campaign's interest in demonstrating public support with individuals' right to privacy.

---

## What We Publish

### ✅ Aggregate Statistics
Statistics such as "96% of commenters support the family" or "1,235 public comments analysed" are **fully safe to publish** under GDPR. Aggregate data does not identify individuals and is standard research practice.

### ⚠️ Anonymised Quotes
All commenter names published on this website are **anonymised to first name and last initial** (e.g. "Maria R."). Where possible, quotes are **translated and/or paraphrased** rather than reproduced verbatim, to further protect the identity of the original commenter.

We apply the principle of **contextual integrity**, recognising that a comment posted on a Facebook video was shared in a specific context, and that republishing it elsewhere is a separate act requiring its own privacy considerations.

### ❌ What We Do NOT Publish
- Full names of commenters (unless explicit written consent has been obtained)
- Screenshots of comments with names visible
- Verbatim quotes that could uniquely identify a person without further anonymisation
- Internal analysis reports containing personal data

---

## Named Quotes & Consent

Where a quote is published with a full name on this website, we have obtained **explicit written consent** from that individual. Our consent process involves:

1. Contacting the individual directly (via private message or public reply requesting a private conversation)
2. Explaining how their words would be used
3. Receiving clear affirmative consent  
4. Retaining a record of the consent given

---

## Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are a commenter whose words appear (in any form) on this website, you have the right to:

- **Request removal**, We will remove your quote promptly
- **Request access**, You may ask what data we hold relating to you
- **Request rectification**, You may ask us to correct any inaccuracy
- **Object to processing**, You may object to our use of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact:

📧 **[privacy@quantummerlin.com](mailto:privacy@quantummerlin.com)**

We aim to respond to all requests within **72 hours**.

---

## Data Storage & Retention

- Internal audience intelligence reports are stored securely and are **not published**
- Published data on this website contains only anonymised or consented information
- Data is retained only as long as it is needed for the campaign's legitimate purpose
- Data will be securely deleted when it is no longer required
